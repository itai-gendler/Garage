# Registration

A vehicle's tag and registration.

## Information kept

- **License plate** — the plate or tag number
- **State** — the state or province it is registered in
- **Expiration** — when the registration must be renewed
- **Renewal fee** — what renewal costs
- **Notes** — the Owner's notes
- **Photo** — a photo of the registration card
