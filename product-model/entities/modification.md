# Modification

An upgrade or accessory fitted to a vehicle, such as a lift kit or aftermarket wheels.

## Information kept

- **Title** — what was fitted
- **Category** — Performance, Suspension & Steering, Exterior & Body, Interior & Comfort, Lighting, Wheels & Tires, Audio & Electrical, Exhaust or Other
- **Description** — details, part numbers and installation notes
- **Installation date** — when it was fitted
- **Cost** — what it cost
- **Product link** — a link to the product
- **Photos** — up to six photos
