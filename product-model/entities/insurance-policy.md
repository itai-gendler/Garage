# Insurance policy

The insurance policy covering a vehicle.

## Information kept

- **Provider** — the insurance company
- **Policy number** — the policy's number
- **Expiration** — when the policy must be renewed
- **Premium** — what the policy costs
- **Agent contact** — agent or claims contact details
- **Notes** — coverages and notes
- **Photo** — a photo of the insurance card
